1. What are the most popular colors by season?  
Most popular colors in winter were: Aubergine, Black, Brick Red, Brown, Burnt Orange, Mauve, Muted Mustard Yellow, Ruby Red and Terra Cotta, with each approx 125 purchases. 

Spring followed with Baby Blue, Fuchsia, Lavender, Lemon Yellow, Pale Peach, Periwinkle, Turquoise and White, with approx 70 purchases each. 

Summer had similar patterns to Spring, with Baby blue, Fuchsia, Lavender, Lemon Yellow, Pale Peach, Periwinke, Turqoise and White, with higher totals approx 100 each color. 

Fall had similar companions to winter as well, finishing with Aubergine, Black, Brick Red, Burnt Orange, Mauve, Muted Yellow, Ruby Red and Terra Cotta, with approx 100 items purchased for each color.

2. What is the most popular clothing item by season?
The most popular item in the winter was surprisingly Sunglasses, with 312 purchased. Then in the spring the hoodie was the most popular at 202 purchases. Summer had running shoes at the top with 309, and finally in the fall the most dresses purchased was 294. 

3. What is the effect of promo codes on the dollar amount of purchases?
I was unsure how to analyze the relationship with purchase amount and promo codes here, so I decided to find the average Purchase amount in USD for whether the customer used a promo code or not, and compare the results. The average customer spent $34.41 on each purchase without using a promo code, while the average customer who did use a promo code spent $44.41 on their purchases. Based on these findings, it is possible that those who used a promo code were more likely to spend more money than those without a code. 
4. When do users leave a review?
It's strange, it seems to be that age was the only factor that looks like it effected when users leave a review. There were no reviews available for those over the age of 30/31. The rest of those who left no review didn't disclose their age either. It seems like the vast majority of reviews only came from those 30 or under. I tried sorting by promo codes, previous purchases, etc, but could find nothing else that could potentially affect when users left a review. Note: It is possible that this is wrong due to clerical errors.
